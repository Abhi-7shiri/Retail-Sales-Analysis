# E-Commerce Sales Analysis Portfolio (2014-2017)

## Project Overview
An interactive data analytics portfolio tracking dynamic e-commerce business metrics including **$2.30M in total sales**, profit metrics, order volumes, and categorical performance. This project leverages a combined Excel and Power BI workflow to clean raw transaction datasets, uncover regional distributions, analyze category profitability, and isolate product-level growth drivers.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
 **Total Sales:** $2.30 Million
 **Total Profit:** $286.40 Thousand
 **Overall Profit Margin:** 12.47%
 **Total Orders Processed:** 5,000+ orders

## Core Visualizations & Analytical Focus
 **Sales & Profit Trend Analysis:** Modeled seasonal and yearly growth metrics (2014–2017) demonstrating a steady upward trajectory in revenue.
 **Geographical Distribution:** Evaluated regional market share showing **West ($0.73M)** and **East ($0.68M)** as the primary revenue-driving regions.
 **Category Performance:** Segmented transactions to isolate **Technology** as the top-performing category by sales volume.
 **Discount vs. Profit Correlation:** Scatter plot profiling of sub-categories to uncover how high average discounts affect profitability margins (e.g., assessing low-margin outliers like Tables and Bookcases).
 **Product Pareto Analysis:** Highlighted the Top 10 revenue-generating products, led by the *Canon imageCLASS 2200 Advanced Copier*.

## Tech Stack Used
 **Data Visualization & Interactive Dashboarding:** Power BI 
 **Data Preparation, Cleaning & ETL:** Advanced Excel (XLOOKUP, Pivot Tables, Power Query)

## Key Business Insights Derived
 **Regional Dominance:** The West region leads all territories with $0.73M in sales, showing strong customer density and market penetration.
 **Category Driver:** Technology is the primary engine of high-value growth, outperforming Furniture and Office Supplies in total margin generation.
 **Discount Optimization Opportunity:** Sub-category analysis shows high discounting strategies on heavy furniture segments directly depress the overall 12.47% profit margin floor.
